# Memo: refunds for INC-2026-031

From: Ana Beridze, Finance Operations. To: Executive team. Date: 23 March 2026.

## Summary

Finance Operations has completed the refunds for the duplicate charges of 18 March 2026. In total we refunded 1,317 duplicate charges, for a total of EUR 191,236.50. The last refund was issued on 20 March 2026.

## How the refunds were done

We followed the duplicate charge refund runbook. Each refund used reason code DUPLICATE and went to the second charge of the pair. Payouts of the 212 affected merchants were held in the 19 March settlement run and released on 20 March.

## Costs

The refunds return money to cardholders and are not a loss for NovaPay. Acquirer refund fees for these transactions are still being confirmed with the acquirer.

## Open points

- Monthly merchant statements for March will show each duplicate charge and its refund as separate lines.
- Merchant Support will answer cardholder questions with the standard refund template.
