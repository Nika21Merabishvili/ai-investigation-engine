# Email to affected merchants: duplicate charges on 18 March

Subject: Duplicate charges on 18 March 2026 - refunds completed
Sent: 20 March 2026 by NovaPay Merchant Support (Sofia Marin)
Recipients: the 212 merchants affected by INC-2026-031

Dear merchant,

On 18 March 2026, a technical problem at NovaPay caused some of your customers' card payments to be captured twice. You did nothing wrong, and no action is needed from you.

Every duplicate charge has now been refunded to the cardholder. The refunds appear in your dashboard with the reason "Duplicate - NovaPay". Your payout for 19 March was held so that duplicate money would not be paid out, and it was paid together with your 20 March payout.

We have changed how long payment retries are protected against double charging, and we are adding load tests before every fraud model change.

If a customer asks about a second charge, they can expect the refund on their card statement within 3 to 5 business days.

We are sorry for the trouble this caused you and your customers.

NovaPay Merchant Support
