# NovaPay status page - 18 March 2026

Public updates posted on the NovaPay status page during the payments incident.

## 10:45 UTC - Investigating

We are investigating delays and timeouts when creating card payments. Some merchants may see HTTP 504 errors.

## 11:20 UTC - Identified

Starting at 10:15 UTC, some card payments were captured twice after a timeout. We have identified the cause and applied a fix. New payments are processing normally.

## 13:15 UTC - Resolved

The incident was resolved at 13:05 UTC. We will contact affected merchants directly, and every duplicate charge will be refunded. We apologise for the disruption.
